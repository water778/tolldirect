## Mapping to the questions asked

1. Passenger-funded credit without first debiting the driver balance: No.
2. Credit before or with a delayed post: No. Funds must be in the account at the time of travel.
3. Insufficient balance during delay: If funds were present at the date and time of travel, the toll posts and the account may go negative because of that post. This does not authorize a later third-party credit.
4. Payment file or credit interface: Not recognized. Tolls do not exist before they post. Nothing to credit. The account holder must keep a positive balance at all times.
5. Adjustments, refunds, duplicates: Commercial adjustment is the private-to-commercial difference. Refunds go to the original payment method or by MassDOT check. Failed replenishments are refunded within a day. Duplicates can be disputed.
6. New arrangement or paper pilot: Account terms and conditions cannot be changed or amended.
