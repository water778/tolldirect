Subject: Request for a feasibility discussion — institutional funding of a driver’s E-ZPass MA account

Dear EZDriveMA Commercial Department,

Please route this note to the MassDOT official responsible for EZDriveMA commercial accounts and the Transportation Network Company toll procedure under M.G.L. c. 159A½. This is a request for a discussion only. It is not an application, a procurement response, or a request to implement anything.

Our question is not how to redirect a driver reimbursement. The goal is to remove the driver’s obligation to advance passenger tolls from personal funds.

Today, a toll incurred during a rideshare trip is posted to the driver’s E-ZPass MA account. The driver must already have funded that account. The passenger pays the toll once, inside the trip fare, and the transportation network company later reimburses the driver. We are asking whether a different arrangement is even discussable: an approved TNC, or a payment provider acting for that TNC, would fund the driver’s registered E-ZPass MA account directly. The toll portion would not pass through the driver’s bank account, and the driver would not be paid that same toll.

We understand the current published process. Account holders replenish their own accounts. Your terms restrict assignment without written consent. TNC ride data is used to upcharge the driver’s account to the commercial rate, and that adjustment can take up to two weeks. We are not asking to change that process now.

We would like to know whether MassDOT would discuss the following:

1. Could an approved TNC fund a driver’s registered E-ZPass MA account through an institutional payment arrangement?
2. Could that arrangement include a prefunded balance, an authorized credit, or another advance-funding mechanism, so the driver does not have to finance the passenger toll before the account is credited?
3. Is there any existing institutional, fleet, or dedicated settlement account that could support this, or would a new written arrangement be required?
4. If a new arrangement would be required, which office would decide whether it can be discussed, and what contract path would apply?
5. How would delayed toll postings be handled, so a late post does not produce a Pay By Plate invoice or require the driver to advance funds in the meantime?
6. How would the commercial-rate adjustment interact with an institutional credit, including the reported delay of up to two weeks?
7. How would refunds, failed credits, and duplicate payments be prevented or reversed, so the rider is not charged twice and the driver is not both credited at E-ZPass and reimbursed?
8. Would MassDOT be willing to review a paper pilot limited to one approved TNC, E-ZPass MA accounts, and Massachusetts facilities, with no live payments until written approval?

No account numbers, credentials, or funds are included. Please direct us to the correct office if this is not it.
