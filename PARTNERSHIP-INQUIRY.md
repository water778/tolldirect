We are studying whether a passenger’s toll portion could, under a future written arrangement, be credited to the driver’s registered E-ZPass MA account. The driver would not receive that toll and would not advance it from a personal bank account. The rider would not be charged twice.

We understand the current process. Account holders replenish their own accounts. Your terms restrict assignment without written consent. TNC ride data is used to upcharge the driver’s account to the commercial rate, and that adjustment can take up to two weeks. We are not asking to change that process now.

We would like to know whether a discussion is possible on these points:

1. Could MassDOT consider an authorized third-party credit to a named E-ZPass MA account?
2. Is there any existing institutional, fleet, or dedicated settlement account that could receive such a credit?
3. If neither exists, which office would decide whether a new arrangement can be discussed, and what contract path would apply?
4. How would a future credit need to interact with the TNC commercial-rate upcharge and with Pay By Plate treatment if posting is delayed?
5. Would MassDOT be willing to review a paper pilot limited to one TNC, E-ZPass MA accounts, and Massachusetts facilities, with no live payments?
