Dear EZDriveMA Commercial Department,

Please route this inquiry to the MassDOT official responsible for EZDriveMA commercial accounts and the Transportation Network Company toll procedure. This is a request for information. It is not an application, a payment, or a request to change any account.

We are evaluating a proposed arrangement called TollDirect. The question is whether MassDOT could, under a future written agreement, accept an authorized credit to a driver’s registered E-ZPass MA account for an eligible toll incurred during a rideshare trip. The passenger would pay the trip fare once. The toll portion of that fare would be allocated to the driver’s E-ZPass MA account. The driver would not receive the toll and would not replenish that toll from a personal bank account.

We understand the current published process is different. EZDriveMA account holders replenish with a card, bank account, check, or in-person payment. Your terms place that duty on the account holder and restrict assignment without express written consent. Your rideshare page states that TNC data is used to upcharge the driver’s account to the commercial rate, and that the driver must keep the account funded.

Before any pilot is considered, we need to know:

1. Does MassDOT permit any authorized third party to credit a named E-ZPass MA account, other than the account holder’s own card, bank, check, or in-person payment?
2. If not, which office would have to approve a new arrangement, and what procurement or contract path applies?
3. Is there any private API, payment file, or approved integration for account credits? If not, what interface would MassDOT require?
4. How should a proposed credit interact with the existing TNC commercial-rate upcharge, including the reported delay of up to two weeks?
5. What timing does MassDOT require so a delayed credit does not produce a Pay By Plate invoice or a non-renewal mark?
6. Would a limited paper pilot be considered, restricted to E-ZPass MA accounts and Massachusetts facilities, with no live payments until written approval?

We are not asking Uber, Lyft, or any payment provider to act on this letter. No credentials, account numbers, or funds are included. Please reply with the correct contact if this office is not the right recipient.
