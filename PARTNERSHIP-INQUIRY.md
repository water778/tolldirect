Dear EZDriveMA Commercial Department,

Please route this note to the MassDOT official responsible for EZDriveMA accounts and the Transportation Network Company toll procedure under M.G.L. c. 159A½.

This is a request for a technical and institutional feasibility discussion only. It is not an application, a procurement response, or a request to implement anything. TollDirect is a project name. It is not presented here as a registered company, and it has no existing partnership with MassDOT, Uber, or Lyft.

We are not requesting any change to how Massachusetts tolls are calculated, classified, or rated. We are asking whether a new passenger-funded settlement mechanism is even discussable.

The proposed mechanism is narrow. During an active rideshare trip, a gantry crossing would be matched to that trip. The passenger would be the sole source of payment, using the payment method already authorized for the trip. That payment would be directed to the E-ZPass MA account associated with the vehicle. No additional passenger action, application, or second charge would be required.

TollDirect does not ask Uber, Lyft, MassDOT, or the driver to finance the passenger toll. The driver would not advance it, and neither company would advance it.

The central question is whether this trip-linked credit can be applied without first debiting the driver’s existing E-ZPass balance.

We understand the current process. The gantry read posts to the driver’s account. The driver must already have funded that account. The passenger pays inside the trip fare, and the company later reimburses the driver. Official posts are delayed, a zero balance can produce a Pay By Plate invoice, and the commercial-rate adjustment can take up to two weeks. We are not asking to change toll calculation. We are asking whether passenger funds could cover the same posted toll before the driver’s balance is drawn.

Could MassDOT clarify the following?

1. Can a passenger-funded, trip-linked credit be applied without first debiting the driver’s E-ZPass MA balance?
2. If the official post is delayed, can that credit be applied before or with the post?
3. What happens if the balance is insufficient during the delay? Is there any confirmed period before a Pay By Plate invoice or penalty?
4. What office, payment file, or interface would receive such a credit, and what confirmation would be returned? Is any such interface available today?
5. How would a later commercial-rate adjustment, refund, failed credit, or duplicate crossing be handled so the passenger is charged once?
6. Would a new written arrangement be required, and which office would decide whether a paper pilot could even be discussed?

No account numbers, credentials, or funds are included. Please direct us to the correct office if this is not it.

Thank you for considering a discussion.
