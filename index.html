How it works

  1. Passenger pays the trip fare once.

    The toll is already inside that fare. TollDirect does not create a second charge.

  2. The toll portion is identified.

    A future official rideshare feed would mark the toll component of the fare.

  3. That portion is routed to the driver’s E-ZPass account.

    Settlement would go to the designated transponder account, not through the driver’s personal bank.

  4. The driver does not recharge from a personal bank account.

    Low balance is covered by fare toll portions already collected from riders.

Before launch

This flow is a design prototype. It requires official agreements with the rideshare platform, the E-ZPass or toll authority, and a licensed payment provider before any real routing can happen.

Partner / compliance

Required before any live payment

Rideshare platform

E-ZPass / toll authority
