How it works

Not a driver reimbursement app.

TollDirect is a proposed direct toll-payment allocation and settlement system. The passenger-funded toll portion goes to the driver’s registered E-ZPass account.

  1. Associate the trip with the driver’s E-ZPass account.

    A future official feed would match the active trip, vehicle, and registered transponder. Ending digits only are shown here.

  2. Determine the eligible toll amount.

    Only the toll already inside the single passenger fare. No second charge. Ineligible tolls stay out.

  3. Route it through authorized TollDirect payment routing.

    Passenger → authorized routing → E-ZPass account. The driver never receives or forwards the toll.

  4. Credit the E-ZPass account and reconcile.

    Confirm the account credit, not a driver payout. Failures, delayed toll posts, and refunds are tracked. No personal-bank fallback.

Timing

Existing E-ZPass replenishment is often not instant. Virginia E-ZPass describes automatic replenishment as usually within 24 hours of a low balance. Toll posts can also arrive later than the gantry crossing. Real-time credit is unconfirmed until an agency offers it.

Partner / feasibility

What exists today

Uber and Lyft currently reimburse the driver. Lyft states it does not pay toll collectors and that drivers pay agencies directly. That is the opposite of this model. No public E-ZPass API was found for a third party to credit a specific driver account.

Required agreements

Rideshare platform

Official change from driver reimbursement to allocation of the fare’s toll portion to a registered E-ZPass account. Must block a second rider charge and a driver payout of the same toll.

E-ZPass / toll authority

Official account-credit path, account-to-trip association, posting confirmation, delayed-toll handling, and refunds. Agency-by-agency. No assumed real-time credit.

Licensed payment provider

Authorized movement of the allocated toll portion. TollDirect does not hold or move money in this prototype.

No live payments

No live payments until those approvals exist. Buttons only change simulated status. Failures do not fall back to the driver’s bank.
